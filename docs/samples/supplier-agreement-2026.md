# Master Services Agreement 2026 — Northwind Logistics (sample document)

This is a sample document for trying NEXUS. The companies and figures are fictional.

## Term and renewal

The agreement runs for 36 months from 1 February 2026. It renews automatically for 12-month periods unless either party gives 90 days' written notice before the end of the current term.

## Fees

The monthly service fee is €13,000 for the first 24 months and €14,200 for the remaining 12 months. Fees are invoiced monthly in advance and are due within 30 days of the invoice date. Late payments accrue interest at 1.5% per month.

## Termination for convenience

The customer may terminate for convenience with 60 days' notice. A termination for convenience in the first 24 months triggers an early-exit fee equal to 35% of the fees remaining in the initial term. After month 24 the early-exit fee falls to 15% of the remaining fees.

## Liability

Each party's total liability under the agreement is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The cap does not apply to breaches of the data protection clause, to gross negligence or to wilful misconduct, which are uncapped.

## Data protection

The supplier processes shipment data on the customer's behalf and must report any personal-data breach within 24 hours of discovering it. The supplier must keep data in the European Economic Area unless the customer approves a transfer in writing.

## Service levels

The supplier commits to 99.5% monthly availability of the tracking platform. For each full 0.5% below the target, the customer receives a service credit of 5% of that month's fee, up to a maximum of 25%.
